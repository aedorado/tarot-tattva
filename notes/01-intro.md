## 2. What can Tarot predict?

### 2.1 The doubt people have
- Many people feel the cards look like ordinary playing cards ("tāś ke patte") and wonder what prediction can come from them.
- Comparison given by the teacher:

| Subject | What the reader has |
|---|---|
| Astrology | Date, time and place of birth → chart → planets, daśā → easy prediction |
| Numerology | Date of birth → root numbers (e.g. 2, 3), then grid and daśā |
| Palmistry | The hand; fingerprints differ for everyone |
| Tarot | "Nothing": she never asks a client for date of birth or birthplace, only **name and question** |

### 2.2 Scope
> [!IMPORTANT] Teacher's claim
> *Teacher stated:* any question can be answered with Tarot: timing questions (when will I marry, when will a child come, why is it not happening / the reason), choice questions (admission in school A or B), today's day, even **one hour of the day**.

- Because of this, **Tarot readers' fees are very high**.
- Even monthly predictions on **YouTube** are often accurate, *if* done with that energy. The students will also try it themselves later.
- **Live predictions:** In other subjects, practical work starts about six months after learning. Here, **once the cards are completed, predictions can start from the next day**.

### 2.3 Tarot is energy-based
- Tarot is an **energy-based** card; energy is something we **feel**.
- **Analogy 1 (patient):** a patient in hospital begins to recover; the doctor advises taking him home because a change of energy and surroundings will change his health. We are changing the person's energy by changing the environment.
- **Analogy 2 (home):** same curtains, same bedsheets for long → we change them → our energy and feeling change.
- Is **Reiki or Pranic** (or any energy form) needed to align with the energy? **No, not at all.** If it comes, good; it is not compulsory to bring it into Tarot.

### 2.4 "Divination tool" (first explanation)
- Tarot is called a **divination tool**. The teacher explained: it is a **daivī vidyā** (divine science). Astrology, Tarot, palmistry are all *daivika vidyā*: you can learn them only when **God's grace** is on you ("otherwise you would have been learning earlier").
- Prediction (past, present, future) is called "a very small thing". Bigger: you can **guide** someone.
  - In astrology we guide by remedies and by "choose this path".
  - In Tarot we guide through the cards, including **what path is better** and remedies.

> [!EXAMPLE] Admission question
> - **Question:** Where should my son take admission: Bangalore or Rajasthan?
> - **Teacher's point:** no other science can tell this except Tarot.
> - **Answer in the example:** Rajasthan; the school will be good and the child will be happy there.
> - **Reasoning/Outcome:** this is the biggest guidance: the child will get a good environment. Tarot works as a **guiding tool**.

### 3.2 Karma theory (three karmas)

> [!INFO] The three karmas
> 1. **Sañcita karma:** a **box** of the fruits (results, good or bad) of all deeds done in earlier births. We know every karma gives a fruit; no karma is without fruit. We don't know what is inside.
> 2. **Prārabdha:** a **portion** of the sañcita that comes to us in this life as our destined part; how much is known only to God. In astrology it is seen from the **fifth house**. ("Go Meenu, this is your prārabdha; your life will run like this.")
> 3. **Kriyamāṇa karma:** our **present karma** (e.g. the teacher teaching, the students learning; going to a job, coming back, caring for children, cooking).

| Karma | Can remedy change it? |
|---|---|
| Sañcita | No (already done; "no question of changing") |
| Prārabdha | **No**; anything connected with the fifth house cannot be changed |
| Kriyamāṇa | **Yes**; remedies are used **only and only** for kriyamāṇa |

- **Tools** (astrology, Tarot, praśna śāstra, numerology, palmistry, any healing subject) are used to **improve kriyamāṇa karma**: they tell past, present, future and guide.
- *Speaker emphasized repeatedly:* you cannot change prārabdha ("not even this much"; "no one can change it").

> [!EXAMPLE] Bad daśā coming (kriyamāṇa improved)
> - If I learn a bad daśā is coming, I start **saving money** from today because health, job or business may be affected.
> - The daśā and circumstances did not change, but my **perspective** towards the situation changed.

> [!EXAMPLE] Numerology family example
> - **Who:** child with root number **2** (emotional); father root number **1**; mother root number **6**. (Root number = from date of birth.)
> - **Interpretation:** the child can be handled emotionally; scolding makes him cry and he will not share. Number 1 father: ego, anger, discipline. Number 6: "family love" number, very family-oriented.
> - **Reasoning:** each handles the child in their own way because **their energy is like that**; the child receives both in his own way due to his own energy.
> - **Outcome:** a family problem can be resolved if you are **guided correctly**.
> - People say "numerology stopped my divorce". Teacher: not really remedies from numerology: **your nature changed**. Example given: when the child comes from school, sit with him, talk, draw, colour; the child feels "Papa is talking to me so nicely". When we understand the situation and change ourselves, the situation **automatically changes**.

### 3.3 Deśa-kāla (place and time) in Tarot
- Any prediction today must consider **deśa-kāla**; it is used in astrology and numerology, and **also in Tarot**. "Without deśa-kāla we see nothing."
- Marriage and relationship questions will be the most common ("I don't get along with this one / that one").
- Society changes: 50 years ago everyone got along; today people do not; in 10–15 years someone may not want to see another's face. Thought and perception change.
- The biggest help of these subjects is not just prediction, but the **connection with the client** and **how we feel that energy** while guiding him; then the tool is a "very big helping tool".
- The teacher: we will take Tarot as an **energy-based** subject. (She noted she had not yet reached Tarot proper; only theory about how any of these subjects is used.)

## 4. Origin and history of Tarot

- Tarot is **not new** ("not recently in limelight"); it is a very old subject.
- *Teacher stated:* the **oldest deck** is kept in a **US library**; a deck of **1397**. (She said the deck's name is in the notes.)
- *Teacher stated:* first used in **Egypt**; the **Gypsies** started using it. Earlier it was used like **playing cards**, and the pictures were **not the same as now**; the current pictures are different.
- There are **more than 3000 decks** (probably more now).

> [!WARNING] Terminology
> We call what we hold a **deck**, not "cards" (teacher's instruction).

- **Rider–Waite** is the **original deck** (stated as "Riders and Weight" in the transcript); many copies were made later. It is the one students have bought, and the one used for prediction.
- Other decks are **psychic cards**: a message is hidden inside; whether you can predict with them or not is another matter, but they work as a message. Examples: love cards, **Krishna card, Shiv card** etc. You may use any such card in your readings if you wish.
- *Teacher stated:* 15th century **Egypt** — used like ball-gazing / playing cards; around the **17th century** it came to **India**. Indians are more religious, so much research was done; when you Google, India gave **Tarot the definition of a divination tool**.

### 4.1 Divination tool, second explanation
> [!IMPORTANT] Meaning
> Tarot = a **medium to connect with the Divine**. God will not phone and tell "Meenu, your day will be like this", so we made a **medium**: through the card, my Īśvara/prakṛti guides me about what will happen.

- Comparisons: dreams that give signals; **praśna śāstra** (online classes exist): look at the client's first step, which hand he moved, what action; or the events around you → this is **ETP, Event Time Prediction**.
- **Śakuna śāstra (śakuna–aśakuna):** elders said don't go out if someone sneezed, a cat crossing the path means something. We called it **superstition (andhaviśvāsa)** and stopped listening. **Nature always speaks.**
- **Bhagavān = prakṛti:** teacher's break-up of the word: *bha-ga-vān* — **bhūmi, gagana, vāyu, nīra** (and ākāśa). So God is nature; nature gives indications again and again; we don't hear. "Trust and try it": when you start listening, nature starts answering. Hence "divination tool". (She was unsure how much students believe; asked them to try.)
- **"Royal way of life":** *Teacher stated:* breaking Tarot: **tar = way of life**, **ros = royal**.
  - Teacher clarified: it does not mean learning Tarot makes me royal; it will **guide** me, problems will be sorted and reduced; if I can manage problems, **life may become royal**.

## 5. Myths and truths about Tarot

> [!WARNING] Myth 1: You should not predict for yourself
> - Also said about astrology (don't see your own chart). **Teacher: not true.** You can predict for yourself **provided you are not biased**. Bias = "my answer must be a yes; no negativity". If biased, the negativity may enter you.
> - Follow the guidance given; then you can predict for yourself.

> [!WARNING] Myth 2: You must not buy your own cards; they should be gifted
> - Heard on YouTube. In foreign countries gifting Tarot is a custom; in India (and some other countries) it is not. You would wait forever and never learn.
> - **Teacher: leave this; order online and learn.**

## 6. Why Tarot is easy: pictures, colours, numbers

- Each card has **pictures, colours and numbers**.
- **Analogy:** we remember a movie scene or a song (a film actor, an actress) from childhood but not a maths sum or tables above 10. The reason: the **subconscious mind understands pictures very fast**.
- So Tarot is easy: the mind reads the picture and **you will never forget it**.
- Astrology takes **many years**; in Tarot: **78 cards, finish, then prediction starts**: live, instant and "very good" predictions, because the card has picture, numbers and colours the subconscious reads easily.

## 7. The five principles on which Tarot works

> [!SUMMARY] The five (in the teacher's order)
> 1. **Śakuna śāstra** — indication of nature
> 2. **Numerology**
> 3. **Colours**
> 4. **Symbols**
> 5. **Intuition**

| # | Principle | What the teacher said |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Śakuna śāstra (indication of nature)** | Good/bad; connection with the Divine. First we must connect with the Divine; our tool is Tarot. When predicting, **separate yourself from the client's situation**: don't decide in the mind "he will surely get the job"; the guidance comes from God. This matters most when predicting for yourself (health, finance next six months), where we become biased and want only good cards. Start **listening to nature's indications** from today. |
| 2 | **Numerology** | Not taught in this course (it is a big course; Anjali-ji has been studying it four months). A **very small part** is used: each card has a **Roman number** on it (1, 2, 3, 22, 21 as she listed). The number is associated with a **planet**, because every number's vibration matches a planet. |
| 3 | **Colours** | Cards contain yellow, white, blue etc. Every colour has its own **energy** and a **hidden message**; we decode it for the situation. |
| 4 | **Symbols** | Every card has a symbol/picture: a bag, a flower, a dog etc. Prediction is made from the symbol + colour + number. |
| 5 | **Intuition** | Same card, but your intuition says something else, or the **first thought on seeing the card** is your first answer. |

- **On intuition:** the teacher will not say "wake at 4 a.m. and meditate". If it is God's will, intuition will grow by itself; **no extra effort is needed**. Just follow rule 1: listen to nature's indications; things will "talk to you". Example: you are going somewhere and suddenly stumble; start listening to **why** it happened and you will get the indication.

## 9. Arcana: Major and Minor

> [!INFO] Arcana
> **Arcana = mystery.** The two parts are **Major Arcana** (bigger mystery) and **Minor Arcana** (smaller mystery). "Big events and small events: it can be like this."
> (Names may differ a bit in other sources; she warned students might face some difficulty at the beginning.)

| | Major Arcana | Minor Arcana |
|---|---|---|
| Cards | **22** (numbered **0 to 21**; the first card is **The Fool**, marked **0**; counting 0 gives 22) | **56** (78 − 22) |
| How to identify | Card has a **number on top and the card's name written at the bottom** | Only a **Roman number on top**; **nothing written below** |
| Order of teaching | **First**: Major Arcana complete | Then Minor Arcana |

- Homework note (teacher): **take out the 22 Major Arcana cards** and keep them separate before the next class. Next class starts with Major.
- *Teacher noted:* after further division of the Minor cards (14 each), some cards may show a number on top **and** writing below.

### 9.1 Division of the 56 Minor cards
- 56 is again a big number, so it is divided into **four parts**: 56 ÷ 4 = **14 cards in each part** (a student answered 14). Each part is called a **suit**.
- Teacher showed physical cards on camera to explain the pictures. Example shown: **Five of Cups** (Roman number on top, nothing below → Minor card).

| Suit | Picture on the card (as she described) | Element (final, as taught) |
|---|---|---|
| **Suit of Cups** | A **cup / goblet-like vessel (pyāla)** drawn in the card. Example card: **Five of Cups** | **Water** |
| **Suit of Wands** | A **log / piece of wood**, brown colour (card shown on camera) | **Fire** *(teacher first said earth, then corrected herself to fire)* |
| **Suit of Swords** | **Swords (talwār)**; may be straight or inverted. Example: **Ten of Swords**: a person with many swords in his back | **Air** |
| **Suit of Pentacles** | A **star (sitārā)** shape | **Earth** |

> [!NOTE] Self-correction recorded
> While listing the elements she said "Wands: earth", then corrected: "here earth will not come, here fire in Wands". Later, in answer to a student, she confirmed **Wands = fire**.

- Each suit is tied to an element; she will use the **element for prediction** later and will teach elements further.

> [!EXAMPLE] Element in a reading (quick example)
> - **Question:** Will I get a job in the future?
> - **Card drawn:** element is **water** (card from Cups).
> - **Interpretation given:** water → **emotions**. So you can say the job will be one where you **connect emotionally**; you will feel good there, a **homely feeling**.
> - **Reasoning:** connect the element's meaning to the answer.
